4. 광고

이 게임은 Google AdMob 광고를 보여 줍니다. 보상형 광고는 직접 눌렀을 때만 나오고, 밤 마감 영수증 뒤에 전면 광고가 하루 한 번 나올 수 있습니다. 광고는 개인 맞춤이 아닌 광고로 요청합니다. Google 광고 데이터 처리: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
기기 설정에서 광고 식별자를 초기화하거나 맞춤 광고를 끌 수 있습니다 (Android: 설정 → Google → 광고).

5. 권한

사진·연락처·위치·마이크·카메라 같은 민감한 권한을 요청하지 않습니다.

6. 아동

이 게임은 어린이를 대상으로 만들지 않았으며, 누구에게서도 개인을 알아볼 수 있는 정보를 받지 않습니다.

기기 안 저장분은 앱을 지우면 사라집니다. 이용 통계는 Google Analytics 기본 보관 기간이 지나면 자동으로 지워집니다. 통계 삭제를 원하시면 아래 메일로 요청해 주세요.

Chichi Camp — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-22 · Contact contact@dumbgamelab.com · Developer: Dumb Game Lab

1. Summary

We do not collect information that identifies you — no name, email, phone number, address, photos, contacts, or precise location. There are no accounts, no sign-in and no purchases. Your progress (fields, placed furniture, guest friendships, coins) is stored only on your device and is never uploaded. We collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the game and show ads, as described below.

2. What we collect

(Google Analytics for Firebase / GA4)		 App open/close, daily progress and stage, whether an ad was watched to the end, device model, OS version, approximate country, an app-instance identifier (a random value that does not identify a person), and app error logs To find where players stop playing so we can fix the game

We do not use these statistics to identify anyone, and we do not sell them.

3. Third parties

Analytics and ad data are sent to Google LLC (Firebase, AdMob) for the purposes above. Google’s policy: policies.google.com/privacy

4. Ads

This game shows Google AdMob ads. Rewarded ads appear only when you tap them, and an interstitial ad may appear at most once a day after the nightly receipt. Ads are requested as non-personalized ads. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads).

5. Permissions

We do not request sensitive permissions such as photos, contacts, location, microphone, or camera.

6. Children

This game is not directed at children, and we do not collect personally identifiable information from anyone.

7. Retention and deletion

On-device data is deleted when you uninstall the app. Analytics data is deleted automatically after Google Analytics’ default retention period. To request deletion of analytics data, email us below.

8. Changes · 9. Contact

If our practices change, we will post the update on this page first. Contact: contact@dumbgamelab.com
